Race engineer

Voice engineer for F1 25. Everything is stored on this machine.

1. Gemini (free)

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. No card needed. On the free tier Google may use the audio to improve their products.

2. Radio button on your wheel

F1 25 swallows keyboard input while it has focus, so the engineer listens to a button on your wheel instead. Pick your Moza base below, then hold the button you want.

No button bound yet.

Use a spare face button, not a shift paddle or clutch. Afterwards, remove that button from your F1 25 controls so the game does not also act on it.

What to set in F1 25
  • Settings → Telemetry Settings
  • UDP Telemetry: On
  • UDP Format: 2025
  • UDP IP: 127.0.0.1 (same PC as the game)
  • UDP Port: 20777 (match the box above)
  • UDP Send Rate: 20Hz or higher
  • Graphics: Borderless so the overlay stays visible
